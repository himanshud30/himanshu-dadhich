# Walkthrough: Restructuring Himanshu Dadhich's Website into a Conversion-Led Creator–Studio Ecosystem

We have redesigned and restructured the homepage and data architecture to implement the exact 14-part sequence inspired by [Yūgen Agency's](https://www.yugenagency.com/) commercial clarity, while strictly preserving Himanshu Dadhich's quieter, observational voice and sandstone, paper, and indigo aesthetic.

---

## The 14-Part Revised Homepage Architecture

| Section | Sequence Item | Implementation & Strategic Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **01** | **Hero** | **Clean, uncluttered positioning:**<br/>*Role:* Creative strategist, filmmaker and founder building a globally distributed studio from Jodhpur.<br/>*Supporting Line:* I find the central story, turn it into a creative system and help it travel through content, design and film.<br/>*Two CTAs:* `Explore the Work` & `Start a Conversation`. Uncluttered by early mentions of newsletter, services, or Sundarkand. |
| **02** | **Proof Rail** | **Compact proof bar immediately beneath Hero:**<br/>`7+ years` &middot; `60K+ views on TOOLS` &middot; `Strategy, film and content` &middot; `Based in Jodhpur`<br/>Plus verified partner marks (Scaler, FanCode, CoinDCX, BlueStone, VFS Global, Setu India). |
| **03** | **Selected Work** | **Three curated deep-dive case studies** (*Setu India*, *Rich Torque*, *Tickle & Me*).<br/>Each study features the 8 outcome dimensions: The situation, What was unclear, The central insight, What was proposed, What was created, What changed (commercial lift), What was learned, and visual evidence. |
| **04** | **The Method** | **Find the story &rarr; Build the system &rarr; Make it travel**.<br/>Three clear phases detailing narrative discovery, operational Content OS construction, and cinematic high-retention distribution. |
| **05** | **Signature Engagements** | **5 Structured Service Packages** with Duration, Best For, and Primary Outcome:<br/>1. *The Diagnosis* (1–2 weeks)<br/>2. *The Story System* (4–6 weeks)<br/>3. *The Content OS* (6–8 weeks)<br/>4. *The Creative Partnership* (3 months+)<br/>5. *Original Series* (custom) |
| **06** | **How Working With Me Works** | **6-step visible roadmap** removing commercial uncertainty:<br/>1. Share the context &rarr; 2. The first conversation &rarr; 3. The diagnosis &rarr; 4. The direction document &rarr; 5. Choose the engagement &rarr; 6. Build and document. |
| **07** | **Original Thinking** | **4 core thinking pillars** (*How We Learn*, *How We Live*, *How We Work*, *Creative Strategy*) + **TOOLS Reel Proof Card** (`TOOLS` &middot; `60K+ views` &middot; `1,000+ saves` &middot; `1,000+ shares` &middot; *"One idea about learning that became a piece people wanted to keep."*). |
| **08** | **Studio (TLR)** | **TLR as the delivery apparatus** through which client work is delivered from Jodhpur (Principal-Led Strategy + Agile Specialist Guild). Features Himanshu's real annotated editorial board photograph. |
| **09** | **Original Project** | **Sundarkand: The Inner Odyssey** treated as a sacred cultural undertaking (68% milestone, acoustic Indian classical orchestration, synthetic cinema pipeline, non-commercial patron circle pathway). |
| **10** | **From the Field** | **Field Notes & Dispatches** from Jodhpur, desert civilizations, and vernacular architectural inquiries. |
| **11** | **Results & Client Voices** | **5 small editorial stories** of tangible transformation:<br/>1. *A series became the channel’s strongest-performing format*<br/>2. *A strategy post was shared by the founder being studied*<br/>3. *An internal content problem became a repeatable system*<br/>4. *A brand conversation became a full strategic document*<br/>5. *A cultural idea became a 2.5-hour AI musical film project*<br/>Plus direct quotes from verified partners. |
| **12** | **FAQ** | **12 serious commercial clarity answers** addressing fit, pricing, Himanshu vs TLR, international delivery, production capabilities, client limits, and initial calls. |
| **13** | **Conversation** | **Humanised final conversion** with Himanshu's real portrait, the exact invitation text (*"Let’s begin with the real problem..."*), and **6 qualifying conversation paths** (`I have a brand problem`, `I want to create a series`, `I want to support Sundarkand`, `I have a question for Himanshu`, `I want to collaborate`, `Something else`) leading to `Share Your Project`. |
| **14** | **Footer** | **Permanent footprint:**<br/>`JODHPUR, INDIA — WORKING WORLDWIDE`<br/>Location-status indicator: `JODHPUR / BASE` &middot; `WORLD / FIELD` &middot; `CURRENTLY / ONLINE`. |
